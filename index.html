How it works

Annual salary = hourly rate × hours per week × weeks worked per year. Monthly pay is annual divided by 12, weekly is hourly rate × hours per week, and daily assumes hours per week are spread evenly across 5 workdays.

Real take-home pay depends on your country, filing status, deductions and benefits. Use this calculator for planning, not as tax advice.
